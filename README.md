## 🛠️ Featured Software & Tools

### 🎛️ mvc2stereo
A specialized utility designed to streamline stereoscopic 3D post-production workflows.
* **Purpose:** Converts Multiview Video Coding (MVC) H.264 formats into standard stereoscopic layouts.
* **Target Workflows:** DaVinci Resolve 3D editing, spatial video mastering, and XR headset deployment (Apple Vision Pro, Meta Quest 3, PICO 4).
* **Documentation & Download:** Visit the [mvc2stereo Tools Page on lab3d.kw.ac.kr](https://lab3d.kw.ac.kr).

## 🚀 Research & Industry Focus

Our lab bridges the gap between academic R&D and live industry deployment through high-profile masterclasses and corporate partnerships:

* **Virtual Production:** Active pipeline development using Unreal Engine 5, Mo-Sys tracking tracking systems, and D.KE hardware.
* **Spatial Computing:** Creating optimized stereoscopic delivery frameworks for modern MR/XR hardware displays.
* **Industry Training:** Organizing major cinematic technical workshops, including our recurring masterclasses at the **Busan International Short Film Festival (BISFF)** and the **MBC High Tech Center**.
